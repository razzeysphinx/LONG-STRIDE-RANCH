Head Trainer

Danielle Harrity

Danielle brings 40 years of riding and 25 years of management and business development experience to Long Stride. She grew up riding competitively, managed a show barn after college and trained with European Olympic medalists. She returned to horses full time in 2019 after 14 years directing events and new projects for Blue Hill restaurants in New York.

Operations & Finance

Sam Harrity

A lifelong competitive horseman, Sam oversees operations and finance at Long Stride. He brings more than 25 years of experience in finance and capital markets, with a background in expansion, new business lines and structured finance.
