# Photography wishlist

## Horse portraits

**Individual horse head portraits** — Vertical, 4:5; Our Horses and sales profiles. Clean natural light, horse looking just off camera. Creates recognition before specifications.

## Action / jumping

**Full horse and rider over a fence** — Landscape, 3:2; home and training. Leave room around ears, legs, rails, and rider. Supports competition storytelling.

## Horse + rider

**Quiet partnership at rest** — Vertical, 4:5; boarding, About, contact. A close, unforced connection to make care tangible.

## Quiet care

**Hands-on care moment** — Vertical, 4:5; boarding. Grooming, checking in, or a calm stable interaction; shows attention without inventing amenities.

## Grooming

**Detail of grooming tools and horse** — Square, 1:1; future editorial details. Texture and care without decorative ranch styling.

## Tack

**Tack preparation** — Square, 1:1; future training story. Clean, working context rather than a product shot.

## Barn and turnout

**Authentic barn aisle and turnout** — Landscape, 3:2; boarding. Show atmosphere and everyday environment without claims about facilities.

## Wellington and Wyoming

**Authentic seasonal context** — Landscape, 3:2; seasonal story. Only use locations Long Stride actually operates from; avoids generic stock geography.

## Danielle and Sam

**Natural working portraits** — Vertical, 4:5; About. One image each in the work environment, with room for editorial crops.

## Manuka Honey

**Head portrait and full-body standing shot** — Vertical, 4:5 and 5:6; sales profile. Natural light, simple background, full ears and feet visible.

## Video

**Short, quiet observational clips** — Vertical 4:5 and landscape 16:9; future social / profile use. Horse at rest, flatwork, and a complete jump sequence; no autoplay required.
