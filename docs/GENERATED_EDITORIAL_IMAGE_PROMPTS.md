# Long Stride Ranch — Generated Editorial Image Prompts & Manifest

This document specifies the approved prompt templates for **Editorial (Category B)** media.

### Critical Boundary Guidelines

1. **Never Impersonate Long Stride Reality**: Editorial images must focus on macro tactile details (leather, reins, coats, grooming hands). They must never portray or invent a fake Long Stride barn, stall, property, arena, or person.
2. **Never Impersonate Named Horses**: Specific horses (such as Manuka Honey) must strictly use documentary photography.
3. **Purpose**: Editorial images exist exclusively to evoke equestrian sensory tactility ("I can feel the leather and reins", "I can almost hear the arena").

---

## 1. Horse Eye (Calmness & Soul)

```text
Extreme editorial close-up of a bay horse's eye, eyelashes and fine coat texture, calm expression, soft natural morning light, shallow depth of field, realistic equine anatomy, high-end English equestrian editorial photography, understated natural colors, no identifiable facility, no branding, vertical 4:5.
```

- **Intended Aspect Ratio**: 4:5 (Vertical)
- **Target Context**: `/horses` (The Partnership / Quiet Moments), Homepage (`Horsemanship`)
- **Emotional Intent**: Quiet intelligence, calmness, trust.
